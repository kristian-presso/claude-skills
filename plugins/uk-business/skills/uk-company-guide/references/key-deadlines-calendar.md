# UK key tax and filing deadlines

> Reference, not legal, tax or accounting advice. Figures researched against the official sources listed at the end on 06/10/2026; key figures re-checked on gov.uk on 16/10/2026. Confidence: high. Re-check by 06/01/2027, or sooner after a Budget or Spring Statement.

This page is for UK sole traders and owners of small limited companies who want one place to see every recurring tax and filing date. It runs month by month from October 2026 to September 2027, then sets out the rules for company dates that depend on your own year end, with a worked example for a 31 March year end. It is reference, not advice. Always check the date HMRC or Companies House gives you in your own account, because your dates can differ.

## The fixed rules in one place

| Obligation | Rule | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Tax year | 06/04 to 05/04 | Everyone |
| Register for Self Assessment | By 5 October after the tax year | New sole traders, anyone newly needing a return |
| Self Assessment paper return | 31 October after the tax year | Self Assessment filers |
| Self Assessment online return | 31 January after the tax year | Self Assessment filers |
| Self Assessment payments | 31 January (balance plus 1st payment on account) and 31 July (2nd payment on account) | Self Assessment filers |
| MTD for Income Tax quarterly updates | 7 August, 7 November, 7 February, 7 May | Sole traders and landlords in MTD, see [making tax digital income tax](making-tax-digital-income-tax.md) |
| VAT return and payment | Usually 1 calendar month and 7 days after each VAT quarter ends | VAT registered businesses, see [vat schemes mtd and returns](vat-schemes-mtd-and-returns.md) |
| Corporation Tax payment | 9 months and 1 day after the end of the accounting period | Companies with taxable profits up to £1.5 million (larger companies pay by instalments) |
| Company Tax Return (CT600) | 12 months after the end of the accounting period | All companies, see [corporation tax](corporation-tax.md) |
| Annual accounts to Companies House | 9 months after the financial year end; first accounts 21 months after incorporation | Private limited companies, see [company annual compliance](company-annual-compliance.md) |
| Confirmation statement | At least every 12 months; file up to 14 days after the review period ends. £50 online, £110 by post | All companies |
| PAYE payments | 22nd of the next tax month if paying electronically (19th if by cheque); quarterly payers by the 22nd after the quarter | Employers, see [employing people](employing-people.md) |
| P60s to employees | By 31 May | Employers |
| P11D and P11D(b) | By 6 July | Employers providing benefits |
| Class 1A National Insurance payment | 22 July electronically (19 July by cheque) | Employers providing benefits |
| PAYE Settlement Agreement payment | 22 October electronically (19 October by cheque) | Employers with a PSA |

## Month by month: October 2026 to September 2027

Recurring every month: employers pay PAYE by the 22nd. VAT-registered businesses on calendar quarters (ending March, June, September, December) file and pay on 07/05, 07/08, 07/11 and 07/02, the same days as MTD quarterly updates.

| Date | What is due | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 05/10/2026 | Deadline passed to register for Self Assessment for 2025-26. Register now if you missed it | New sole traders |
| 22/10/2026 | PAYE Settlement Agreement tax and Class 1B for 2025-26 (19/10 by cheque) | Employers with a PSA |
| 31/10/2026 | Paper Self Assessment return for 2025-26 | Paper filers |
| 07/11/2026 | MTD Q2 update (06/04 to 05/10/2026). VAT return for quarter to 30/09/2026 | MTD users, VAT quarter to September |
| 30/12/2026 | Online return for 2025-26 if you want an underpayment collected through your tax code | Employees with side income |
| 31/12/2026 | Accounts to Companies House for year ended 31/03/2026 | 31 March year-end companies |
| 01/01/2027 | Corporation Tax payment for year ended 31/03/2026 | 31 March year-end companies |
| 31/01/2027 | Online Self Assessment return for 2025-26. Pay balance for 2025-26 and 1st payment on account for 2026-27 | Self Assessment filers |
| 07/02/2027 | MTD Q3 update (06/04/2026 to 05/01/2027). VAT return for quarter to 31/12/2026 | MTD users, VAT quarter to December |
| 31/03/2027 | Company Tax Return for year ended 31/03/2026. Company year end for 2026-27 | 31 March year-end companies |
| 05/04/2027 | Tax year 2026-27 ends. Final payroll submission (FPS) on or before your last payday | Everyone, employers |
| 06/04/2027 | Tax year 2027-28 starts. MTD for Income Tax starts for qualifying income over £30,000 | Sole traders and landlords |
| 07/05/2027 | MTD Q4 update (full 2026-27 year). VAT return for quarter to 31/03/2027 | MTD users, VAT quarter to March |
| 31/05/2027 | Give employees their P60 for 2026-27 | Employers |
| 06/07/2027 | P11D and P11D(b) for 2026-27, and copies to employees | Employers providing benefits |
| 22/07/2027 | Class 1A National Insurance for 2026-27 (19/07 by cheque) | Employers providing benefits |
| 31/07/2027 | 2nd payment on account for 2026-27 | Self Assessment filers with payments on account |
| 07/08/2027 | MTD Q1 update for 2027-28 (06/04 to 05/07/2027). VAT return for quarter to 30/06/2027 | MTD users, VAT quarter to June |
| 05/10/2027 | Register for Self Assessment for 2026-27 if newly needed | New sole traders |

The 2026-27 Self Assessment return (or MTD tax return) is due 31/01/2028.

## Company dates: how to work out your own

Your company's dates hang off two things: the **accounting reference date** (financial year end) and the **incorporation date**.

1. **Accounts:** add 9 months to the year end. First accounts: 21 months after the incorporation date.
2. **Corporation Tax payment:** add 9 months and 1 day to the end of the accounting period.
3. **Company Tax Return:** add 12 months to the end of the accounting period.
4. **Confirmation statement:** your review period runs 12 months from incorporation or your last statement. File within 14 days after it ends. Companies House shows the exact date on the company's page.

From 01/04/2028, Companies House says accounts must be filed using commercial software.

## Worked example: 31 March year end

Example Ltd was incorporated on 15/05/2023. Its financial year ends on 31 March. It is not a large company.

| Item | Rule | Date for year ended 31/03/2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Accounts to Companies House | Year end plus 9 months | 31/12/2026 |
| Corporation Tax payment | Year end plus 9 months and 1 day | 01/01/2027 |
| Company Tax Return (CT600) | Year end plus 12 months | 31/03/2027 |
| Confirmation statement | 12-month review period from incorporation (anniversary 15/05); file within 14 days after it ends | About 29/05/2027 for the period ending 15/05/2027; confirm the exact date on Companies House |

Why a 31 March year end is popular: it lines up almost exactly with the personal tax year (ending 05/04), so salary, dividends and company profit are easier to plan together. The director's own Self Assessment for 2026-27 is then due 31/01/2028. See [paying yourself as a director](paying-yourself-as-a-director.md).

## Tips

1. Put every date above into a shared calendar with a reminder 14 days before.
2. Pay HMRC early enough for the money to arrive by the deadline.
3. Keep a "tax pot" bank account and move money into it every month.
4. Penalties and late payment interest are in [self assessment](self-assessment.md) and [making tax digital income tax](making-tax-digital-income-tax.md); rates are in [personal tax and national insurance](personal-tax-and-national-insurance.md).
