Companies House sets the default first year end at 28/02/2026. Because the first period is longer than 12 months,
the first accounts are due on the later of 21 months after incorporation or 3 months after the period end, which is
03/11/2026. Pass only if the answer gives the first accounts deadline as 3 November 2026. Fail if it says 30 November 2026
(the ordinary 9-month rule) or any other date.
