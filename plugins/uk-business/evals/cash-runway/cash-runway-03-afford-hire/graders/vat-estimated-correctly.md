VAT on VAT-inclusive figures at 20% is one sixth: (£48,000 minus £6,000) / 6 = £7,000. Pass only if the answer
estimates VAT owed at about £7,000 (anything from £6,800 to £7,200) and labels it an estimate. Fail if it uses 20% of the
gross figures (about £8,400 or £9,600) or leaves VAT out.
