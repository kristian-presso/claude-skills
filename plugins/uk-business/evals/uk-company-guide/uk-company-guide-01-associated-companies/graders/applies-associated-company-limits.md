With one associated company the Marginal Relief limits are divided by 2: £25,000 and £125,000. Tax is 25% of
£120,000 = £30,000 less Marginal Relief 3/200 x (£125,000 minus £120,000) = £75, so about £29,925. Pass only if the answer
applies the associated company rule (halved limits) and arrives at about £29,925. Fail if it gives £28,050 (ignoring the
associated company) as the answer, or otherwise does not adjust the limits.
