# Research template

## Per-account entry (for up to about 15 accounts)

```markdown
### <Company name> (<website>)

- **Fit:** <industry, size, location> - <why it matches the ideal account>
- **Signal:** <what happened> - Source: <URL> (<date published>)
- **Further evidence:** <optional second signal> - Source: <URL> (<date>)
- **Contact:** <name>, <role> - Source: <URL> (<date seen>)
- **Alternate:** <name or role> - Source: <URL> (<date seen>)
- **Use case:** <signal -> problem -> how the offer helps, one or two sentences>
- **Inferences:** <anything assumed rather than sourced>
- **Opening line:** <one sentence, built from the signal>
- **Freshness:** <days since signal> - **Priority:** high / medium / low
```

## Filled example (fictional company)

```markdown
### Northgate Clinics (northgate-clinics.example)

- **Fit:** private physiotherapy group, 6 sites, north of England - matches "multi-site
  clinics, 5 to 20 locations, UK".
- **Signal:** announced a seventh site opening in York in November - Source:
  northgate-clinics.example/news/york (03/10/2026)
- **Further evidence:** two receptionist roles advertised for the York site - Source:
  northgate-clinics.example/careers (05/10/2026)
- **Contact:** Operations Director (named on the team page) - Source:
  northgate-clinics.example/team (seen 14/10/2026)
- **Alternate:** Practice Manager, York (role only; no name published)
- **Use case:** a new site with new front-desk staff means rebooking and no-shows are
  handled by people still learning the system; the offer automates reminders and
  rebooking across all sites from one place.
- **Inferences:** assumes the current booking system is not shared across sites
  (not confirmed).
- **Opening line:** "Saw York opens in November - congratulations. Are the new front-desk
  team going to be on the same booking set-up as the other six sites?"
- **Freshness:** 13 days - **Priority:** high
```

## Compact table (for longer lists)

| Company | Signal (source, date) | Contact (source) | Use case | Fresh (days) | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Example Ltd | Series A, company blog, 24/09/2026 | COO, named in post | Doubling headcount, no onboarding owner | 22 | High |

Keep source URLs in the table even when space is tight. A claim without a source is
removed before the list is used.

## Before handing off

- [ ] Every signal has a URL and a date.
- [ ] Every contact has a source, or is a role with no name.
- [ ] Inferences are labelled.
- [ ] Suppression list and existing customers checked.
- [ ] Nothing collected from behind a login or from personal channels.
- [ ] Top accounts ranked by signal strength and freshness.
