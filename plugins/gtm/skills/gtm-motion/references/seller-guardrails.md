# Seller guardrails

Reference, not legal advice.

An employed seller's customer list, pipeline, pricing and internal plans belong to their
employer, and many employers restrict putting them into outside AI tools. Ask before
using any of it, and work from public information if the answer is no or unknown.

## At intake, ask once

1. "Does your employer allow you to use outside AI tools with customer or pipeline data?"
2. **Yes** - note what is allowed and use only that.
3. **No or not sure** - work from public information only: news, company websites, public
   profiles. Account names the seller types in are fine; exports from the employer's CRM
   are not.
4. Record the answer in `gtm-motion.md` so later runs respect it.

## On every draft

- Written as the seller, in their voice, signed by them.
- Every claim about the product matches what the seller confirmed or the company
  publishes.
- Every claim about the buyer has a source, or reads as a hypothesis.
- An opt-out line where the channel and the recipient's country require one.

## Never

- Import an employer's CRM export without the seller confirming it is allowed.
- Impersonate a colleague, a customer or the employer's brand.
- Invent social proof: no made-up case studies, logos, quotes or numbers.
- Work around the employer's sales rules - territories, approved messaging, compliance
  review come first.
- Send anything. The seller approves and sends every message.
