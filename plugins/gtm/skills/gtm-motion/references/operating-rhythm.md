# Operating rhythm

A motion is a weekly loop, not a one-off plan: sweep, retier, run plays on the top
accounts, log what happened, and change one thing.

## The week

| When | What |
| --- | --- |
| Monday | signal sweep, retier, top five accounts with why now |
| Monday to Tuesday | build or refresh plays for new tier-1 accounts (`account-play`) |
| Through the week | the seller approves and sends; replies are logged |
| Friday | five-minute review: what moved, and one change for next week |

Once a month, review the ICP and signal weights against results.

## The numbers to watch

| Number | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Tier-1 accounts with an active play | coverage of the accounts that matter |
| People engaged per tier-1 account | multi-threading, the usual failure in territories |
| Positive reply rate, signal-led versus not | whether timing is adding anything |
| Meetings booked | the output the motion exists for |
| Days from signal to first touch | signals decay in days |

Set the seller's own baseline in the first four weeks and compare against that, not
against vendor benchmarks.

## Changing the motion

Change one thing at a time - a weight, a hypothesis angle, the entry role, the asset -
and hold it for two weeks unless it is plainly failing. Do not measure emails sent:
volume rises easily and says nothing about whether the right accounts are moving.
