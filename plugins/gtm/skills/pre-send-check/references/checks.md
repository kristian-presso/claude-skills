# Pre-send checks

Run all of these before writing outreach, not after. They come from a real pilot where an
automated sweep chose the right accounts, but none of five drafts was sendable as
written: two repeated or contradicted what had already happened, two had wrong dates or
missed what the customer was waiting for, and one had no deliverable address.

Finding the right account and knowing what is already true about it are different jobs.
These checks do the second.

## The checks

| # | Check | How | If it fails |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Already in touch?** | Search your sent mail and calendar for the company's domain and the contact's name, last 14 days. Do not rely on the CRM alone; activity there is often missing. | Drop the draft. If a thread is open, note "let the thread land". |
| 2 | **Meeting coming up?** | Search the calendar for the company in the next 14 days. | No cold email. Turn it into prep for that meeting instead. |
| 3 | **What already happened commercially?** | Search your mail for renewal, auto-renewal, order form, invoice and contract notices for the company. Trust a dated notice over the account data. | Rewrite the premise. Never write "before the renewal" about something that has already renewed. |
| 4 | **Do we owe them something?** | Search call notes, meeting recaps and your own notes for open questions or promised follow-ups. | Lead with closing those loops; the new point goes second. |
| 5 | **Whose account is it?** | Check the CRM owner of the account and contact, and search for recent handover or reassignment documents. | Skip if another person owns it. If ownership is unclear, keep the draft but put the question first in the notes. |
| 6 | **Are the dates right?** | Check every date and weekday in the draft against the calendar invite or source. | Correct it. Never state a weekday you have not checked. |
| 7 | **Can we reach them, and how?** | Find a real address in the CRM, mail history or invites. Note if the relationship runs through a shared chat channel instead. | No guessed addresses. For chat channels, produce paste-ready text; never post it for the user. |
| 8 | **Opted out?** | Check opt-out, unsubscribe and do-not-contact flags in the CRM and sequencing tool. | Drop permanently. |

## For brand-new prospects

Checks 3 and 4 usually come back empty. Checks 1, 5, 7 and 8 matter most: a "new"
company is often already a customer, an open opportunity, or someone else's account.

## Recording the result

Add a **Checks** section to every draft, one line per check: passed, failed and why, or
not possible (and why). A draft with an unexplained gap in its checks is not ready for
review.
