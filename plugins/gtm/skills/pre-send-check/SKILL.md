# Pre-send check

Automated research is good at choosing who to contact and bad at knowing what is already
true about them. This skill closes that gap. Run it on any outreach draft, your own or
one another skill wrote, before it is sent.

The checks, what to look at and what to do when one fails are in
`references/checks.md`.

## How to run it

1. **Get the draft and the target**: company, person, channel, and the draft text.
2. **Run each check** in `references/checks.md` with whatever is connected: the user's
   sent mail and calendar, CRM, call notes, chat. If a check cannot be run, say so and
   why; never mark it as passed.
3. **Act on the results:**
   - **Drop** if they are already in an open thread, have opted out, or belong to someone
     else.
   - **Turn into meeting prep** if a meeting with them is coming up.
   - **Rewrite** if the premise is out of date, a date is wrong, or something is owed to
     them - lead with what is owed.
   - **Change the channel** if there is no real address, or the relationship runs through
     a shared chat channel (paste-ready text; never post on the user's behalf).
4. **Report** one line per check, then the verdict: send as is, send after the shown
   edit, turn into meeting prep, or drop.

## Rules

- A dated notice (an order form, a renewal email, a calendar invite) beats any summary
  or spreadsheet.
- Never state a weekday or date you have not checked against its source.
- Never guess an email address.
- The user sends. This skill never does.
