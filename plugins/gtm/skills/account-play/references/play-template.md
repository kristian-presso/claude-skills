# Play template

```text
Account:        <name>     Tier: <1|2>    Motion: <new-logo | territory | expansion>
Why now:        <top signal, date, source>
Hypotheses:     H1 ...  H2 ...
Entry point:    <name, role, source>   Hypothesis: H1   Channel: <email | social | warm intro | event>
Thread 2:       <name, role>  opens after: <entry replies | week 2>   Hypothesis: H2
Thread 3:       <name, role>  opens after: <week 3>
Asset:          <one useful thing to offer: a short teardown, a benchmark, an invitation>
Exit:           <meeting booked | clear no | 4 weeks, no response>
Next review:    <date>
```

## By motion

- **New-logo outbound** - one thread, one hypothesis, light research. Add threads only
  after a reply.
- **Named territory** - three threads planned up front, spaced so no one gets two touches
  from the seller in the same week.
- **Expansion** - open with the existing champion. The play is about the next team or use
  case, introduced by someone who already trusts the seller.

## Rules

- A play without an exit condition runs forever. Set one.
- A generic brochure is not an asset.
- When the exit is reached with no reply, park the account and wait for the next signal.

## Check

Each play names a why-now with a source, an entry point, at least two further threads, an
asset, an exit and a review date, and every draft carries exactly one ask.
