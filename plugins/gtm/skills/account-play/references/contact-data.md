# Contact data

Reference, not legal advice. Checked 16/10/2026; re-check the sources before relying on it.

Find people from public, professional sources and record where each fact came from. An
email address that names a person is personal data even at work, so UK and EU data
protection applies: have a lawful basis (usually legitimate interests), tell people where
you got their details, honour objections for good, and keep only what you need.

## Finding people

1. The company website: team, leadership, press and speaker pages.
2. Public professional profiles found through ordinary web search.
3. Conference agendas, podcasts, articles and filings that name role holders.
4. The seller's own inbox and past meetings, if their employer allows it.

Record per person: name, role, source URL, date seen, confidence the role is current.

## Getting an email address

| Route | Cost | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No email - social or a warm intro first | free | slower; often better for senior people |
| An address the person or company published | free | rare, but the cleanest basis |
| An enrichment tool the user has connected | paid | higher hit rate; another processor of personal data |
| A guessed pattern, verified before sending | free to cheap | unverified guesses bounce and damage the sending domain |

## UK specifics

- Under PECR, companies, LLPs and some partnerships are **corporate subscribers**: B2B
  email to them does not need prior consent, but the sender must identify themselves and
  give a working opt-out.
- **Sole traders and some partnerships count as individuals** and need consent or the
  soft opt-in. If unsure which an address is, treat it as an individual.
- Keep a suppression list instead of deleting people who object, so they are never
  re-added.
- Since February 2026 the maximum PECR fine is £17.5 million or 4% of global turnover,
  under the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025.

Sources: the ICO's business-to-business marketing guidance
(`ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing/`)
and commentary on the Act's commencement (`bratby.law/duaa-takes-effect-ico-enforcement/`).

## Never

- Scrape or automate on platforms that forbid it. Professional networks such as LinkedIn
  prohibit bots, scrapers and extensions that automate activity.
- Collect personal life details: family, health, home address, politics.
- Keep prospect data forever. Clear contacts with no engagement and no open play.
- Treat "publicly available" as permission. Public data still carries these duties.
