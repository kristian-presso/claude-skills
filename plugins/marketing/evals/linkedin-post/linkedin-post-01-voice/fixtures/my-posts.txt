Post 1:
We lost a £14k wedding booking last spring because nobody answered the phone on a Saturday.

Not because the team was lazy. Because the phone sat in the office and the office was locked.

Now the venue line forwards to whoever is on shift. Cost: nothing. (Took me eleven months to think of it.)

Post 2:
Three things I got wrong in my first year running a venue:

1. Priced by the hour. Clients wanted a day rate.
2. Hid the catering minimum until the quote. People felt tricked.
3. Said yes to every date. Burned out the team by October.

All fixable. All fixed. Still a bit embarrassing.

Post 3:
Our cheapest room is our most profitable one.

It holds 20. It costs almost nothing to turn round. Small teams book it midweek, which is when the big hall sits empty.

I spent two years trying to fill the big hall. Should have been selling the small one.

Post 4:
A client asked why our quotes are one page.

Because the three-page ones were not getting read. We tested it: same price, one page versus three. The one-pagers got a yes about twice as often over six months.

Shorter is not lazier. It is just easier to say yes to.
