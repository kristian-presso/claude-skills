# Stop calling it a 'quick call'

By Dana Whitlock. Published 9 October 2026.

When a supplier asks for a 'quick call', what they mean is thirty minutes of my afternoon. I would rather
they wrote two lines telling me what they want. If it really is quick, it fits in an email. Most of the
'quick calls' I have taken in the last year could have been a three line reply, and the ones that could not
were never quick.
