Hybrid teams rarely get the whole group in one room, so an offsite has to earn its cost. This guide covers
how far in advance to book, what a two-day agenda looks like, and how to budget per head.

## How far ahead should you book?

Book 10 to 12 weeks ahead for groups over 30. Smaller groups can usually book 6 weeks out.

## What does a two-day agenda look like?

Day one is strategy in the morning and team work in the afternoon. Day two is planning and a social.

## How much should you budget per head?

Budget for the room, catering, travel and one evening activity. Get three quotes before you commit.
